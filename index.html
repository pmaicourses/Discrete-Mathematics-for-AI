Discrete Structures for AI

This course introduces the core ideas of discrete mathematics that support reasoning in computing and intelligent systems. Students learn through real scenarios, mathematical representations, proof techniques, combinatorics, graph theory, and selected Python activities. The emphasis is on understanding, problem solving, and connecting mathematical thinking with practical system design.

COURSE OVERVIEW

Course Description

This course develops essential discrete mathematics concepts for students of Artificial Intelligence and computing. It covers logic, proof reasoning, sets, relations, functions, counting, graphs, trees, and related structures through applied examples and problem solving. Selected Python activities are used to reinforce mathematical ideas and connect them with computational thinking.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

Course Learning Outcomes

01

Apply principles of mathematical logic, sets, relations, and functions to solve discrete problems.

02

Construct and evaluate mathematical proofs using appropriate proof techniques.

03

Analyze combinatorial structures and apply counting principles to computational problems.

04

Model and solve problems using graphs, trees, and other discrete structures.
